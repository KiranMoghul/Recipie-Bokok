## Ingredients

### For the sauce
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 400 g tinned tomatoes
- 1/2 tsp salt

### For the pasta
- 320 g spaghetti
- 2-3 cloves garlic, sliced

## Steps

1. Heat the {olive oil} in a pan over medium heat and cook the {onion} for 5 minutes.
2. Add the {tomatoes} and {salt} and simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Cook the {spaghetti} in salted boiling water for 10 minutes, then toss with the sauce.
